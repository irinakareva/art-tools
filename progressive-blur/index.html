Need a reference idea? What makes a good value-shape image

Usually works well

  • 3-6 large value masses still read when you squint or blur the image.
  • Simple background and a small number of large forms.
  • One clear directional light source; side lighting is especially useful.
  • Strong light/dark separation with some soft transitions inside the forms.
  • Large overlapping forms rather than lots of isolated tiny details.
  • For faces/figures: one subject, simple pose, uncluttered background, clear light direction.

Usually harder for this exercise

  • Busy patterns, foliage, crowds, or lots of tiny objects.
  • Dappled or multiple competing light sources.
  • Very flat lighting with little separation between masses.
  • Images whose identity depends on tiny details.
  • Highly textured backgrounds or patterned clothing.
