After printing: how to join the pages

Begin with the first numbered sheet. Remove only the shaded overlap bands from later sheets, align matching image details, and tape on the back.

Shaded/hatched strip = duplicated picture to cut away. Red dashed line = exact cut edge.

How to assemble printed sheets

Hatched shading = the duplicated strip to discard. The red dashed line is your exact cut line. On A2, discard the top strip only when A1 is printed; on B1, discard the left strip only when A1 is printed. An isolated sheet has no neighbor seam to trim.

Gray artwork outline and short midpoint ticks (optional): These show the finished canvas edges and its true horizontal/vertical centers, based on your 12 × 18-inch dimensions. They are independent of the photograph's crop. Keep the artwork outline when positioning the template; only trim the dashed red overlap strips to join sheets.
  1. Print at Actual Size / 100% (not Fit). First measure the 1-inch calibration ruler. Print one-sided.
  2. Lay out sheets using their codes: A1 is top left, A2 directly below it, B1 immediately to the right of A1. The page count may omit intentionally deselected sheets.
  3. For A1 over A2 (when both are selected): Cut off A1's bottom white margin along the edge of its printed image. On A2, cut along its dashed horizontal line, removing the top white margin and the repeated 0.25-inch photo strip. Put the two new edges together. The picture should be continuous.
  4. For A1 beside B1 (when both are selected): Cut A1 at its right image edge. Cut B1 along its dashed vertical line, removing its left margin and repeated strip. Join the two edges.
  5. Secure the pieces from the back with low-tack tape, checking obvious landmarks across each seam. The dashed cutting guides are not part of the final picture.
  6. Position on the canvas: Match the assembled template's gray artwork-edge guides to the canvas edges. Use midpoint ticks to verify that the printed template is centered on the surface. If you printed only A1+A2, you can still align its top and left artwork-edge guides to the top/left of the canvas; the unprinted B region is deliberately absent.
  7. For tracing: Tape the aligned pattern along the top as a hinge, lift it, insert appropriate graphite/chalk transfer paper, and trace the contours. For pouncing, perforate the assembled pattern along the lines first.

Important: If the picture is centered within only A1+A2, it is not necessarily centered within the entire canvas. Choose Center on artwork if you want the subject itself centered on the 12 × 18 surface. If overlap is 0, no repeated strip is present: align the printed image edges without a dashed trim.

The assembled overview numbers the selected sheets in order. The right preview shows one physical printer sheet, with a hatched trim area only where another selected sheet joins. When printing the PDF, select Actual Size / 100% and verify the 1-inch ruler.
