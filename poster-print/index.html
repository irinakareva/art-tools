Art Reference Enlarger

Crop a photo, choose the finished artwork size, and make a multi-sheet PDF with matched overlap and trim guides. All processing happens on this device; create a tiled PDF and share it for printing.

Assembly instructions

  1. Open the PDF and print Actual size / 100%, one-sided, with the selected paper size. Turn off “Fit” and automatic scaling.
  2. Print the first page, measure its 1-inch ruler, and check that it really measures 1 inch before printing the rest.
  3. Arrange printed pages in reading order: A1, B1, C1…, then A2, B2….
  4. At a vertical join, trim the right white margin of the left sheet at the image boundary. On the sheet to its right, cut off the left margin and repeated image strip at its dashed vertical line. At horizontal joins, similarly trim the upper sheet at its bottom image boundary and the lower sheet at its dashed horizontal line.
  5. Align the newly cut picture edges, tape on the back, and trim remaining blank paper outside the outermost artwork boundary. The dashed overlap guides are intended to be removed, not left showing in the assembled image.

For carbon transfer: tape the assembled print over your canvas with transfer paper between them, then trace the important contours. For pouncing: perforate the key contours of this full-size print and pounce chalk or charcoal through the holes.
